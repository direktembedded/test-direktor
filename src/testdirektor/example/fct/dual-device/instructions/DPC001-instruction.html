Place DPC001 PCBA in DPC001-FIXTURE

Careful placement of PCBA into fixture is critical

  • Slide PCBA into Cradle with LEDs at the top right
  • Gently push PCBA down until slider clicks into place
  • Connect ethernet cables ETH_ADAPTER_1 from the front
  • Verify Orange light of cradle is ON
  • Press Start to begin testing
